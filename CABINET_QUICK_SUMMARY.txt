# 🎉 Medicine Cabinet - Complete Rework Done

**Mr. VMJAM**, the **Medicine Cabinet** tab is now fully reworked!

---

## ✅ What Changed

### **New Features Added**

1. ✅ **Refill with Quantity Dialog**
   - Click "📦 Refill" → Prompted: "How many capsules to add?"
   - No more auto +30 (you choose the amount)

2. ✅ **Edit Medication Button**
   - Click "✏️ Edit" → Modal opens with form pre-filled
   - Change name, strength, timing, pills shape/color
   - Save → Updates existing medication

3. ✅ **Delete Medication Button**
   - Click "🗑️ Delete" → Confirm by typing medication name
   - Safety check prevents accidents
   - Medication removed from cabinet

4. ✅ **Scan Label Button (Top)**
   - "📱 Scan Label (Rx or Bottle)" at top
   - OCR scans prescription/bottle
   - Auto-fills form with med details

5. ✅ **Better Font Colors**
   - Medication names: Clear dark text
   - Strength: Teal color (more visible)
   - Instructions: Dark, easy to read
   - Stock info: Stronger font weight

---

## 🎯 Button Layout
